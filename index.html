Welcome!

The meaning behind the name

I’m a neurodivergent web designer and developer and believe that accessibility is not an afterthought but a foundation to the user experience of the world wide web. As such, I build web experiences with accessibility at the forefront of their design and development.

My origin story

I’ve been enamoured by the world wide web since the early days of the internet. I have always been intrigued by how websites function and made to look the way they look. I started learning to code from a young age, and dedicated myself to it full-time in 2020, when I took advantage of the pandemic lockdown to immerse myself in it. I have since developed skills in both frontend and backend development. I also have skills in web design, database development, and web hosting. This makes me a one-stop shop for all your web needs.
