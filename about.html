About me

Some facts about me

  • The first website I ever built took me almost a year. It was a long and excruciating process. I have come a long way since then!
  • For a web developer, I’m fairly disconnected and off the grid.
  • I’m hardheaded. When faced with a problem, I don’t stop thinking about it until I solve it. I don’t know whether this is a strength or a weakness—maybe a bit of both!

How to work with me

  1. Come with an idea about what you’d like your website to look like.
  2. We will work on the design collaboratively so that it is to your satisfaction.
  3. Communicate with me directly if you are not satisfied with any aspect of the design. I highly value direct communication and constructive feedback. At the end of the day, you are the client, and the project is yours.

My greatest weaknesses

  • Past: I spend too much time ruminating on my mistakes.
  • Present: I struggle to manage my time.
  • Future: I struggle to think about my future ambitions.

My future ambitions

To think about my future ambitions.
I’m passionate about community and community-building and highly value the use of digital media as a tool for community-building for and by neurodivergent people. One of my dreams is to build a social media app for neurodivergent people that really addresses our needs in its design—how the user interacts with it and with other users, and the space it takes up in their life.
